## What the 148/148 software tests prove

That selected, declared deterministic behaviors and safety cases work in the local software test environment. They do not prove externally provided records are truthful or that synthetic forecasts would transfer to a new company.

## Completion criterion appropriate for AIMLBT

A tested prototype with evidence-grounded decisions, transparent uncertainty, documented limitations, reproducible experiments and an independently reviewable audit is a defensible completed research deliverable. It is not equivalent to production certification. A future deployment requires additional real source data and independent pilots; claiming all limitations resolved now would be misleading.
